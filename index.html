Rates & defaults
Passcode

The passcode locks this app on your device(s). It is not a substitute for a secured server — see the data note in Help before storing many real customer records in the cloud.

Data

Jobs sync between your devices through your secured cloud database, and a copy is kept on each device for offline use. A downloaded backup is still a good idea now and then.

How it works

New request → Scheduled → Evaluated → To be estimated → Estimate approved → Parts to be ordered → Parts ordered → Parts arrived → Completed. Set a job's status as it moves along; the map colours each pin by status so parts-arrived jobs (green) stand out as ready to go back out.

Parts move the job along: in Estimate & parts, set a part to Ordered and the job becomes Parts ordered; once every part is Arrived the job becomes Parts arrived (a Completed job is never moved back).

Invoice #: made automatically as yymmdd-phone from the received date and the customer's phone (a second job for the same phone on the same day gets -2). You can still type your own.

Paste text/email: top-right button — paste a customer SMS or email and the app pulls out name, phone, address and the problem into a new draft you confirm.

Scheduling: add one or more appointments to a job (evaluation, repair, follow-up). Each has an Add to calendar button (downloads an .ics that imports into Google/Outlook/Apple Calendar) and a Google Calendar quick-add link.

Estimate: labour + parts + wall mount + travel (+ tax) is what you share with the customer. Travel is on the estimate when Include travel on the customer estimate is ticked; the same travel lines still make up the company expense claim. Internal notes never go on the quote.

Credit card fee: tick Paying by credit card in the estimate to add the fee (5% by default, change it in Settings) on top of the whole total.

Payments: add as many as you need (e.g. evaluation fee up front, repair at the end) — the job shows total paid and the balance owing. A prepaid amount ticked in the estimate is taken off the quote total so the customer sees the balance due. Paid jobs show 💵, part-paid ones show what's due, finished-but-unpaid ones show ⚠ Unpaid.

Google Maps: 🧭 buttons (job card, job editor, schedule, map pins) open Google Maps directions — on your phone they open the Google Maps app.

Copy for ServiceBench / Salesforce: the 📋 button beside each field copies just that value.

⚠️ Data & privacy note

Customer names, addresses and phone numbers sync through a dedicated, secured Firebase project that only a signed-in account can read or write. A copy stays on each signed-in device so the app works offline — sign out on any device you no longer use.
